WUMM Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

WUMM ("we", "our") is a wedding planning app for couples, operated by BCD Inc. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name when you sign up with email, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In
  • Automatically collected: device identifier, platform information (iOS), and app usage records
  • Content you create: checklists, budgets, schedules, guest lists, vendor information, wedding invitations, and community posts
  • All data is stored encrypted through Firebase (Google Cloud)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account management and identity verification
  • Couple linking and data sharing between partners
  • Storing your wedding planning data and syncing it across devices
  • Providing community features and managing content
  • Sending push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • App usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)
  • Collecting feedback to improve the service

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers below, to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google)
    Data shared: email, usage data, device information
    Purpose: data storage, sync, push notifications, analytics
    Retention: until account deletion
  • Apple / Google (sign-in providers)
    Data shared: sign-in credentials for the provider you choose
    Purpose: social login
    Retention: until account deletion

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is deleted immediately when you delete your account (except where retention is required by law)
  • Inactive accounts: after 1 year of inactivity, data is stored separately and then destroyed

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information
  • You can edit your name and delete your account in the app's Settings
  • You can turn push notifications on or off at any time
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request its deletion. We honor these requests for all users.

6. Children's Privacy

  • WUMM is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Contact Us

  • Company: BCD Inc.
  • Contact: through the Feedback menu in the app

8. Changes to This Policy

  • If we change this policy, we will notify you in the app or by email
